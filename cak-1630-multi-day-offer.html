Offering one time across several days

“It would also be nice if we could select multiple days at once in the calendar if we're going to offer the same time on those days instead of having to go back and pick up and set the time individually.” ko.kismet, Discord, 17 October · CAK-1630

She already knows the time she wants to offer and the days she is willing to give. Today that is one trip through Add a time per day. Three shapes below, at the width she meets them. The Now frame is the panel as it ships today, for comparison.

The three calls

The issue says this one needs a drawing before code. These are the decisions the drawing is for — my recommendation on each, but the first is genuinely yours.

1. Which shape

A reuses the chip grammar already on this screen and leaves the familiar date field alone. B matches her words more literally and reaches any day, at the cost of a new calendar component on a screen that has already confused people.

I'd build A, and keep “pick another day” as the existing field for anything the chips don't cover.

2. The cap

Eight offered times is the limit. Four days chosen at once is half her allowance, and today going over turns an added row into a held one — a poor way to find out.

Say “4 of your 8” live beside the chips, turn it amber near the limit, and stop accepting taps at eight rather than adding and benching.

3. Days that can't take it

A chosen day may be closed, already booked, or run past her hours. Across several days that could be several reasons at once.

Put the reason on the day itself, before she taps — “Booked”, “Closed” — rather than collecting refusals into a list afterwards. A does this; B only dims.
