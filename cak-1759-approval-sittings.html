Sittings on the approval screen

“what about on the approval time offers screens?” Micah, 2026-10-22 · CAK-1759

Quick add is her booking into her own book, so a list of times there can only mean sittings. Approval is the screen with a client on the other end, and there the same list already means something else.

The whole problem in one sentence. On approval, a list of four times means “four options, pick one”. For a four-sitting piece it would mean “four sittings, all of them”. Same control, opposite meaning — and the client’s page encodes it as a single chosen index (encodeOfferSelection(revision, index, offer)), with a lone offer arriving pre-picked. Get the mode wrong and she either sends four options believing she booked a piece, or books a client into four sittings they thought were choices.

That is the one-tap-send failure again in a new place: an outward message whose meaning the artist misread. So every shape below is judged first on whether the two meanings can be confused.

Never let the two meanings share an unlabelled list

The danger is not that a mode exists; it is a mode that looks the same either way. In A the heading, the ordinals, the button and the line naming what the client is asked all move together.

If only one of those four changed, this would be the one-tap bug again, in a screen that sends mail.

The client page is the real cost

Today it renders radios over timeOffers and posts a single index, and a lone offer arrives pre-picked. A set is a different payload, a different confirmation email, and a different answer to “can I change just the third one?”.

Worth keeping as its own issue rather than hiding it inside CAK-1759.

What “several” does to a refusal

With one sitting, a client who cannot make it asks for new times. With four, they may be fine with three and not the second.

A’s client page should let them flag one sitting rather than reject the set — but that is a decision, not a drawing, and it needs Micah.

One sitting must stay untouched

In A, choosing One leaves the screen byte-for-byte as it is today: “Going to Jordan”, 4 of 8, “Send 4 times”, radios on the client’s page.

The common case cannot pay for the rare one.

Not drawn: a mixed offer

“Three sittings, and for the first one here are two times to choose from.” It is the most flexible and by some distance the most confusing, on both ends.
