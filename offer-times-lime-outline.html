Lime on the added times

Today the lime on a row in Going to Jordan is a wash that lasts four seconds and then goes, and only ever on the one row added last. Below: what it looks like kept as an outline, so a chosen time stays visibly different from an open one. Each frame is the real Times section at 390, in Cake's own tokens.

Studio theme

What to notice

  • Switch the frames to Dark and the whole question changes. Lime against the dark card is loud, and a 2px outline is plenty. Against the light card it is pale yellow on cream — the line is there, but it is the weakest signal on the screen. If the outline has to work in both, A and C are carried by dark and under-read in light; B and D hold in both, B because 3px of solid colour survives low contrast, D because the wash does the work the line cannot.
  • The flash is four seconds, not one — setJustAdded clears after 4000ms. It reads shorter than it is because 22% lime on cream is faint to begin with, which is the same contrast problem arriving early.
  • Only one row is ever marked today. The mark is row.starts_at === justAdded, so adding a second time silently un-marks the first. Any of these options drops that: every chosen time is marked, all the time, which is what "a clear differentiator" asks for.
  • There is already a differentiator, and it is purple. Open times are dashed pills in --acc; chosen times are a solid card. Lime would be a second axis on top of that, so the question is whether lime replaces the purple-dashed language for this pair or sits beside it.
  • The Added badge is worth keeping either way. It is the only part that says just now rather than chosen. If the outline becomes permanent, the badge is what is left to carry the tap's feedback, and it already has a contrast-safe pairing (--on-signal on --lime, both themes).
